What's New in v5.2.0

- Signature Liquid Glass Navigation Bar: Introducing a refractive glass navigation bar with a draggable selector and fluid tab switching across Feed, Stats and Library. Available on Android 13+ with Liquid Glass enabled.

- Adaptive Streaming for Slow Connections: Mobile-data Auto mode adjusts streaming quality to your connection speed and buffer health to help reduce buffering. You can also set separate quality preferences for Wi-Fi and mobile data.

- Faster Startup & Smoother UI: Optimized scrolling, player opening, artwork backgrounds and loading animations while reducing unnecessary redraws and background CPU usage. Artwork and streamed audio now share a configurable cache budget up to 4 GB.

- YouTube Music 59-Second Playback Fix: Resolved stream and recovery issues that could leave songs stuck around 59 seconds or one minute, with improved handling of expired URLs and interrupted playback.

- New Local Music Hub: The Playlists tab is now Library, with dedicated browsing for local songs, albums, artists and folders, offline playlists, local likes and embedded lyrics. Added DSD and AIFF playback support.

- Faster Quality Switching & Hi-Res Controls: Improved mid-song quality transitions with better alignment, fewer jumps and crackling, and a clearer switching indicator. Added an “Auto upgrade to Hi-Res” toggle.

- Refined Glass Headers, Artwork & Videos: Extended scrolling beneath glass headers, improved artwork-based theme colors, and refined synced music-video matching and fullscreen proportions.

- Smarter Shuffle & Reliable Playlist Sync: Shuffle prioritizes unplayed songs. Fixed playlist ordering, duplicate-song removal, YouTube playlist deletion and Spotify sync/count issues, with one heart for local and connected YouTube likes.

- USB DAC & Audio Fixes: Improved exclusive USB volume response, loudness normalization, Dolby gain and Cayin RU3 handling, alongside ALAC DASH playback support.

- Downloads, Accounts & Stability Fixes: Improved download retries, playlist imports and folder permissions, and fixed false Last.fm logouts, older-Android crashes and multiple UI bugs. Added Vietnamese language support.
